Section 02
The Challenge

Sunshine Dental Group had never conducted a formal HIPAA Security Risk Analysis — a requirement under 45 CFR §164.308(a)(1). An OCR audit inquiry triggered urgent action. The practice used cloud-based EHR software, personal devices for scheduling, and had no written information security policies in place.

  • No documented Security Risk Analysis (OCR §164.308 requirement)
  • No written Information Security Policy
  • No Business Associate Agreements (BAAs) on file
  • Personal devices accessing PHI without MDM or encryption
  • No staff security awareness training in 3+ years
  • Shared login credentials in use at front-desk workstations
Section 03
Engagement Approach
1
Phase 1 — Discovery & Risk Mapping · Weeks 1–2
  • Inventoried 14 asset categories (EHR, patient portal, PACS, workstations, mobile, email, VPN, physical records)
  • Mapped 9 threat types against each asset using a 5×5 likelihood × impact risk matrix
  • Identified 23 risk pairings — 3 Critical, 8 High, 9 Medium, 3 Low
  • Assigned risk owners by role: Office Manager, IT Vendor, Dentist-Owner
2
Phase 2 — Evidence & Gap Analysis · Week 3
  • Evaluated all 12 applicable HIPAA Security Rule controls
  • Found 7 controls with zero documentation (Not Started)
  • Found 3 controls partially implemented but completely undocumented
  • Built prioritized evidence collection log with ownership and due dates
3
Phase 3 — Remediation Playbook & Reporting · Week 4
  • Delivered 90-Day Playbook with 3 structured phases and measurable milestones
  • Generated OCR-ready HIPAA Security Risk Analysis PDF with Security Official attestation
  • Delivered non-technical Executive Summary for practice owner decision-making

Section 06
90-Day Remediation Playbook
Phase 1 · Days 1–30
Foundation & Quick Wins
  • Inventory all PHI-touching systems
  • Collect & review all BAAs
  • Enable MFA on EHR, email, portal
  • Access review — remove termed staff
  • Enable screen auto-lockout (all PCs)
  • Patch all workstations & servers
Phase 2 · Days 31–60
Policy & Documentation
  • Draft Information Security Policy
  • Draft Incident Response Plan
  • Draft Data Classification Policy
  • Implement RBAC in EHR system
  • Enable audit logging (all PHI systems)
  • Configure encrypted backup + test restore
Phase 3 · Days 61–90
Training & Validation
  • HIPAA Security Awareness Training (all 22 staff)
  • Collect signed training attestations
  • Tabletop incident response exercise
  • Final evidence review & audit binder
  • Risk register sign-off (Security Official)
  • Submit completed SRA to file
