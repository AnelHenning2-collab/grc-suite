Incident Severity Levels

SEV 1 — Critical
Active breach, ransomware, PHI exposure, system-wide outage. Respond immediately.
SEV 2 — High
Suspected breach, privileged account compromise, major service disruption.
SEV 3 — Medium
Policy violation, malware on isolated system, failed phishing attempt.
SEV 4 — Low
Suspicious activity, minor policy violation, unconfirmed alert.
