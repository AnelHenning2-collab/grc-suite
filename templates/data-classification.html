Data Classification Policy

1. Purpose

This policy establishes a framework for classifying organizational data based on sensitivity and regulatory requirements, and defines handling requirements for each classification tier to ensure appropriate protection.

2. Classification Tiers

🟢 Public
Examples: Website content, press releases, marketing materials, published reports
Requirements: No access restrictions. May be freely shared internally and externally.
🔵 Internal
Examples: Policies, procedures, org charts, internal memos, meeting notes
Requirements: Not for external distribution without approval. Store on internal systems only.
🟡 Confidential
Examples: Employee records, contracts, financial data, vendor agreements, audit reports
Requirements: Encrypt in transit and at rest. Limit access to authorized roles. Log access.
🔴 Restricted
Examples: PHI, PII, passwords, encryption keys, cardholder data, SSNs
Requirements: Strict need-to-know. Encrypt always. Audit all access. Regulatory requirements apply.
