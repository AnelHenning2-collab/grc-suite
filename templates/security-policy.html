Information Security Policy

Logical Coders GRC Suite  |  logicalcoders@outlook.com  |  Aligned: NIST CSF 2.0 · HIPAA · ISO 27001 · SOC 2

1. Purpose

This Information Security Policy establishes the security requirements and responsibilities for to protect its information assets, systems, and data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

2. Scope

This policy applies to:

  • All employees, contractors, vendors, and third parties with access to organizational systems
  • All information systems, networks, devices, and data owned or managed by the organization
  • All data classifications including:

3. Acceptable Use

Organizational systems and data must be used only for legitimate business purposes. The following are prohibited:

  • Unauthorized access to systems, accounts, or data belonging to others
  • Installation of unauthorized software, applications, or browser extensions
  • Sharing credentials, passwords, or access tokens with others
  • Transmitting sensitive data via unencrypted or unapproved channels
  • Using organizational systems for personal financial gain or illegal activities
Edit this list to reflect your organization's specific prohibited activities.

4. Access Control

  • Access to systems and data shall be granted on a least-privilege, need-to-know basis
  • All users must authenticate with a unique username and strong password (minimum characters)
  • Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is required for:
  • Access shall be reviewed quarterly and revoked immediately upon termination or role change
  • Privileged accounts shall be documented, monitored, and limited to authorized IT staff

6. Incident Reporting

All employees must report suspected security incidents immediately to:

  • IT Security contact:
  • Report method:
  • What to report: suspicious emails, unauthorized access attempts, lost or stolen devices, unusual system behavior, known or suspected data exposure

7. Password & Authentication Standards

  • Passwords must be at least characters and include uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols
  • Passwords must not be reused for at least prior passwords
  • Passwords must be changed every days or immediately upon suspected compromise
  • Passwords must not be written down, shared, or stored in unsecured files
  • A password manager is for all staff

8. Remote Work & Mobile Devices

  • Remote access to organizational systems requires MFA and an approved VPN
  • Work must not be conducted on public, unsecured Wi-Fi networks without VPN
  • Lost or stolen devices must be reported within hours
  • All mobile devices accessing organizational data must be encrypted and password-protected

9. Training & Awareness

  • All staff must complete security awareness training within 30 days of hire and annually thereafter
  • Phishing simulations will be conducted
  • Training completion is documented and retained for compliance purposes

10. Policy Violations

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination of employment or contract, and where applicable, legal action. Violations involving protected health information (PHI) may trigger HIPAA breach notification requirements.
