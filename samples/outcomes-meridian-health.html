5. Recommendations for Next Wave

  1. PACS Migration: Accelerate Windows 7 legacy node migration — Q4 2026 funding approved; target completion March 2027.
  2. BYOD: Complete MDM enforcement following legal approval. Deploy MAM containerization for ePHI access on all personal devices.
  3. HICP Implementation: Consider adopting HHS Health Industry Cybersecurity Practices (HICP) as a practical control implementation guide for the next governance wave.
  4. Third-Party Risk Program: Expand vendor risk assessment to all Tier 1 and Tier 2 vendors — not just the billing vendor. Target: 12-month rolling assessment cycle.
  5. Retainer Engagement: Monthly retainer ($3,500/month) for ongoing evidence maintenance, quarterly risk re-assessment, and board-level NIST CSF reporting support.
