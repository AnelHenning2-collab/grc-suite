§164.308(a)(1) — HIPAA Security Rule

Security Management Process

Evidence DescriptionOwnerMethodFrequencyStatus
No written Security Risk Analysis exists. This assessment is the first formal documentation of organizational risk.Dr. Santos (Security Official)InspectionAnnualGap — Missing
⚠ Gap: OCR has cited §164.308(a)(1) in over 90% of investigated breaches. A written, dated, signed Security Risk Analysis is the single most-required evidence item in any HIPAA audit. This must be completed and signed before any other remediation activity. Priority: Immediate.

§164.312(a)(1) — HIPAA Security Rule

Access Control — Unique User Identification

Evidence DescriptionOwnerMethodFrequencyStatus
Current state: All front-desk staff share a single Dentrix login. The dentist and hygienists use separate accounts but passwords are posted on the staff bulletin board. No MFA is enabled on any system.IT ContractorObservationContinuousGap — Missing
⚠ Gap: Shared logins are a direct HIPAA violation. They make audit logs legally useless — you cannot demonstrate which individual accessed which patient record. MFA is not explicitly required by HIPAA but is considered a best practice and is required by most cyber liability insurers. Priority: Immediate — remediate within 14 days.
§164.312(b) — HIPAA Security Rule

Audit Controls — Activity Logging

Evidence DescriptionOwnerMethodFrequencyStatus
Dentrix EHR generates access logs. However, no process exists to review these logs. Logs have not been exported or reviewed in the past 12 months. Retention policy not defined.Dr. Santos (Security Official)InspectionMonthly review (target)In Progress

Evidence to collect: EHR audit log export (last 90 days); documented log review procedure; log retention policy (minimum 6 years per §164.530(j)); assigned reviewer name and review schedule.

§164.312(e)(1) — HIPAA Security Rule

Transmission Security — ePHI in Transit

Evidence DescriptionOwnerMethodFrequencyStatus
Staff currently send patient information, appointment reminders, and referral documents via personal Gmail accounts. No encryption is applied. No Business Associate Agreement exists with Google.All StaffObservation / InterviewContinuousGap — Missing
⚠ Gap: Transmitting ePHI via unencrypted email is a HIPAA violation regardless of whether a breach occurs. Evidence required: HIPAA-compliant email platform subscription (Microsoft 365 Business with BAA, or equivalent); signed BAA with email provider; written policy prohibiting personal email for patient communications. Priority: 30 days.

4. Next Steps

  1. Sign and date the Security Risk Analysis (this document) immediately — this closes the §164.308(a)(1) gap.
  2. Create individual EHR logins for all staff within 14 days. Remove the shared account.
  3. Subscribe to Microsoft 365 Business, execute the BAA with Microsoft, and migrate all patient communications within 30 days.
  4. Establish a monthly EHR log review process and assign a named reviewer.
  5. Complete workforce access authorization forms for all current staff within 45 days.
  6. Re-assess all gap controls at Day 60 and update this report prior to any audit or insurance renewal.
