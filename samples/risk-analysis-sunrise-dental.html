1. Executive Summary

This Security Risk Analysis was conducted for Sunrise Dental Group in accordance with HIPAA Security Rule §164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A), which requires all covered entities to conduct an accurate and thorough assessment of potential risks and vulnerabilities to the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of electronic Protected Health Information (ePHI).

This is the practice's first formal Security Risk Analysis. Prior to this engagement, no written risk assessment existed — a direct HIPAA compliance gap. The assessment identified 5 risk items across the practice's systems, data, and operational processes. Immediate action is required for all Critical and High findings prior to the scheduled renewal of the practice's cyber liability insurance policy.

3. Findings

Critical (1): The Dentrix EHR System faces an unacceptably high ransomware risk due to the absence of endpoint protection, unverified backup integrity, and no tested incident response plan. A successful attack would trigger mandatory breach notification under HIPAA §164.400 and potential OCR enforcement action. This finding must be escalated to the Security Official immediately.

High (3): Staff email, the patient billing portal, and cloud storage each present a high probability of ePHI exposure. The use of personal Gmail for patient communications violates HIPAA's minimum necessary and transmission security standards. Dropbox usage without a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) is a direct HIPAA violation.

Medium (1): Physical access controls for paper records present a moderate risk. While electronic systems are the priority, physical safeguards are a required component of HIPAA Security Rule compliance (§164.310).

4. Recommendations

  1. Immediate: Deploy endpoint detection and response (EDR) on all workstations and verify backup integrity within 14 days.
  2. Immediate: Enable MFA on all staff email accounts and the billing portal. Discontinue shared administrative credentials.
  3. 30 days: Migrate from personal Gmail to a HIPAA-compliant email platform (Microsoft 365 Business with BAA, or equivalent).
  4. 30 days: Execute a Business Associate Agreement with all vendors who access, store, or transmit ePHI on behalf of the practice.
  5. 60 days: Implement a sanctioned, HIPAA-compliant file-sharing platform and prohibit personal cloud storage for work use.
  6. Ongoing: Conduct annual security awareness training for all staff. Document all training completions per HIPAA §164.308(a)(5).
  7. Ongoing: Retain this Risk Analysis for a minimum of 6 years per HIPAA §164.530(j). Re-assess annually or upon any material change.
