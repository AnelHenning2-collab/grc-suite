1. Executive Summary

Thank you for the opportunity to support Sunrise Dental Group. Based on our initial assessment and your intake submission, your practice currently has no formal security program and is operating with three active HIPAA compliance gaps: no written Security Risk Analysis, shared EHR credentials among staff, and patient communications sent via unencrypted personal email.

The initial risk assessment identified 5 risks including 1 Critical and 3 High severity items. Left unaddressed, these gaps create direct OCR audit exposure and potential fines ranging from $100 to $50,000 per violation category. More critically, your practice's cyber liability insurance renewal may be contingent on demonstrating a formal security program.

Logical Coders proposes a 90-Day HIPAA Security Governance Pilot designed to close your critical gaps, produce a written and signed Security Risk Analysis, and deliver an audit-ready documentation package — all structured for a small practice with limited IT resources.

Why This Matters for Your Practice

  • OCR cited §164.308(a)(1) (missing risk analysis) in 93% of investigated breaches in 2024
  • Average HIPAA fine for small practices: $25,000–$100,000 per violation category
  • Average ransomware recovery cost for small healthcare practices: $340,000 (downtime + recovery + notification)
  • Most cyber liability insurers now require a documented security program for coverage renewal

2. Proposed Scope — 90-Day HIPAA Pilot

Phase 1  ·  Days 1–30  ·  Inventory & Risk
Build the Foundation
  • Complete asset inventory: all systems, devices, cloud accounts, and vendors that touch patient data
  • Finalize and sign the HIPAA Security Risk Analysis (addresses §164.308(a)(1) immediately)
  • Identify and document all Business Associate relationships; execute missing BAAs
  • Remediate shared EHR login — create individual accounts with MFA for all staff
  • Present Phase 1 findings to Dr. Santos as Security Official
Phase 2  ·  Days 31–60  ·  Policies & Evidence
Close the Gaps
  • Migrate patient communications to HIPAA-compliant email platform; execute BAA with provider
  • Draft and implement: Information Security Policy, Workforce Security Policy, Acceptable Use Policy
  • Define evidence for all 5 HIPAA priority controls; assign evidence owners
  • Conduct gap analysis update and document all remediation actions taken
  • Deliver Phase 2 status report to Dr. Santos
Phase 3  ·  Days 61–90  ·  Training & Validate
Validate & Sustain
  • Conduct staff security awareness training session (meets §164.308(a)(5))
  • Collect and organize evidence for all 5 priority HIPAA controls
  • Validate evidence quality: complete, accurate, and appropriately retained
  • Deliver final 90-Day Outcomes Report with audit-ready documentation package
  • Recommend next wave priorities and ongoing retainer options
