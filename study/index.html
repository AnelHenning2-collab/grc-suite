Mnemonics & Memory Tricks

Memorize the mnemonic, recall the concept on exam day.

CIA
Confidentiality · Integrity · Availability

The three pillars of information security. Every security decision traces back to one of these.

AAA
Authentication · Authorization · Accounting

Who are you? What can you do? What did you do?

PICRL
Prepare · Identify · Contain · Recover · Lessons Learned

Incident Response steps. Add Eradicate between Contain and Recover.

KHAL
Know · Have · Are · Location

The four authentication factors.

IDS vs IPS

IDS = Camera. Watches. Records. Alerts. Does NOT stop anything.
IPS = Security Guard. Watches AND physically stops the threat.

Trick: IDS Detects. IPS Prevents. D and P tell you everything.

Hot / Warm / Cold Sites

Hot = Engine running, drive immediately. Most expensive.
Warm = Car in garage, 30 min warmup. Moderate cost.
Cold = Empty parking space. Cheapest — days to get operational.

RTO vs RPO

RTO = How long can we be DOWN? (future — when do we recover)
RPO = How much DATA can we lose? (past — how far back does backup go)
